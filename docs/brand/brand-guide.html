Construction

64 grid. Radius 18. Stroke 9, round caps. Points 17,23 → 37,23 → 19,44 → 45,44. Spark r4 at 48,16.

Clear space and size

  • Clear space on every side: a quarter of the mark's height.
  • Smallest mark: 20px. From 16 to 20px, use favicon.svg (stroke 10, spark r5).
  • Smallest lockup: mark at 28px.
  • In RTL layouts, mirror the lockup, never the mark.

Please don't

  • Add gradients, glows or shadows to the tile.
  • Square the stroke ends or change the stroke weight.
  • Move the spark or turn it white.
  • Set "Zakerly" in anything but Readex Pro.

  1. Talk like a senior student. "I read your slides", not "documents indexed".
  2. End with an action. Every error and empty state says what to do next.
  3. Write Arabic, don't translate it. Egyptian register, course terms stay in English.
  4. Never blame the student. Canvas failed, the student didn't.
  5. Be honest about the source. Not in the files? Say so.
  6. Keep it short. One idea per line, one exclamation mark per screen at most.
